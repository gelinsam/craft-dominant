# Automatic coffee action packs

Existing six-hour maintenance now prepares private weekly action packs whether or not the dashboard is open. The launch endpoint reads the latest prepared snapshot and marks it stale after 24 hours. Revision files preserve prior recommendations and evidence; they are not proof a proposed action was executed.

Each upcoming coffee edition receives a phase-based content sequence, localized captions, links to reviewed coffee-account source posts for reuse, adaptation instructions, email copy, reference cases, pacing rationale and paid-support guidance. First launches and relaunches prefer other-city assets. Returning editions prefer their own same-duration history. A prior edition spending more and selling fewer tickets triggers conversion diagnosis, never an automatic budget increase. Source-quality warnings stay visible. Prices, capacities and offer differences are not adjusted by these descriptive comparisons.

The six-city reconstruction supplies historical references. Additional stored coffee markets use existing edition_sibling_ids and aggregate orders, with unavailable paid history explicitly unknown. Exhibitor/vendor/sponsor destinations are excluded. Houston has relative preparation while its event details are missing. Draft content must not claim reused photos document that city's prior event. Source links and editing briefs are supplied; media binaries are not downloaded or rendered, and old names/dates/roasters must be removed during adaptation.

CRM counts reuse build_crm_audience for past buyers, one-and-done buyers and super spreaders. Current buyers are excluded for ticket-sales purpose. Segments overlap and counts are candidates, not provider-confirmed reachable recipients. No emails or contact rows are persisted in packs. Email copy is not an Eventbrite/Mailchimp draft and the generator does not send, post, schedule or modify ads.

Reuse: existing reconstruction, edition identity, CRM eligibility, maintenance callback, atomic private snapshot writer, protected proxy and UI. dlt and DBOS were evaluated for generic ingestion/runtime work; no additional framework is required for these Craft-specific decision rules. No parallel scheduler or autonomous learning claim is introduced.

Nine targeted tests cover cross-city reuse, own-history precedence, event-duration matching, higher-spend/lower-sales diagnosis, unknown/stale data, weekly identities, unsafe URL exclusion, unconfigured Houston, additional-city grouping, vendor exclusion and CRM count-only output.
